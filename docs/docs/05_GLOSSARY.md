# Glossary

## Acquisition
The process of bringing goods into FHS ownership or custody.

## Receiving
Confirmation that physical goods have arrived.

## Inventory
A physical asset managed by IMS.

## Product Definition
The master definition shared by one or more inventory items.

## Sales Session
A selling event such as Facebook Claim Sale, Auction, Direct Sale, or Offline Sale.

## Claim Cart
A buyer-oriented temporary container holding claimed items before checkout.

## Checkout Group
One or more Claim Carts merged into a single payment flow.

## Settlement
Allocation of incoming payment into its accounting components.

## Wallet
Represents financial rights, not bank balances.
