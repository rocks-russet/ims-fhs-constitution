# FIN-BR-021 — Cash Advance and Accountable Fund

Purpose: Control funds issued in advance for approved business activity and ensure complete accountability for their use and return.

Rules:
- A cash advance is money entrusted to a responsible person before the final business expense or transaction is known.
- A cash advance is not an operating expense, reimbursement, withdrawal, or profit distribution at the time it is issued.
- Every cash advance must identify:
  - advance ID;
  - responsible person;
  - funding Wallet Code and bucket;
  - amount and currency;
  - approved purpose;
  - issue date;
  - accountability due date;
  - requester, approver, and executor;
  - Settlement Account or custody reference.
- Issuing an advance must create a traceable accountable-fund position linked to the responsible person.
- The responsible person must account for the advance using valid expense evidence, returned funds, or another approved settlement record.
- Valid expenses supported by the advance must follow FIN-BR-009 and reduce the outstanding advance amount.
- Unused funds must be returned through a traceable receipt linked to the advance.
- The outstanding advance amount must equal the amount issued minus accepted expenses, valid returns, and approved adjustments.
- Additional funds must not be issued against an overdue advance unless an authorized exception is recorded.
- An advance must not be converted into a personal withdrawal or capital contribution through relabeling.
- Loss, shortage, or unsupported use of advanced funds must be recorded as an exception or obligation and resolved under the applicable approval, loss, and recovery rules.
- Completion, cancellation, reversal, and overdue status must preserve the entire accountability history.
