# FIN-BR-074 — Financial Write-Off Policy

## Purpose

Govern the controlled recognition of unrecoverable balances, losses, damaged value, or immaterial residual differences.

## Rules

1. A write-off requires a uniquely identified balance, amount, owner, reason, supporting evidence, and approval.
2. Permitted write-off categories include unrecoverable receivable, inventory loss, damaged inventory value, unrecoverable advance, settlement shortfall, and approved immaterial residual.
3. Reasonable recovery or correction efforts must be documented before write-off, unless immediate write-off is justified.
4. A write-off must use a dedicated financial posting and must not delete or reduce the original source record directly.
5. Write-off approval follows the financial approval matrix and applicable amount thresholds.
6. Inventory write-offs must synchronize inventory status, valuation, ledger impact, and ownership consequences.
7. Recovery received after write-off must be recorded as a separate recovery entry linked to the original write-off.
8. Write-offs involving suspected fraud or control failure must also create a financial exception or incident.
9. Write-off reports must identify gross amount, recovered amount, net loss, category, owner, and approval reference.

## Invariants

- A write-off is not a substitute for unexplained balance adjustment.
- The original obligation or asset remains traceable.
- A later recovery does not erase the write-off history.
