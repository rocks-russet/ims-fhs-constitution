# FIN-BR-031 — Financial Source Documents and Evidence

Purpose: Ensure every material financial record can be traced to sufficient, authentic, and reviewable supporting evidence.

Rules:
- Every financial posting, approval, adjustment, settlement, withdrawal, reimbursement, or reclassification must reference an authoritative source event and supporting evidence appropriate to its nature.
- Supporting evidence may include an invoice, receipt, payment confirmation, marketplace statement, bank or e-wallet record, contract, approval record, shipping document, inventory receipt, or another accepted business record.
- Evidence must identify, directly or through its linked source record:
  - involved parties;
  - transaction date;
  - amount and currency;
  - purpose or economic substance;
  - external or internal reference;
  - responsible actor;
  - relationship to the resulting ledger record.
- A screenshot, image, or copied text must not be treated as authoritative when a stronger original record or provider statement is reasonably available.
- Evidence files must preserve their original content; annotations, classifications, and review notes must be stored separately or as non-destructive metadata.
- Replacement of evidence must preserve the prior file or reference, replacement reason, actor, timestamp, and approval when required.
- Missing, incomplete, contradictory, or unverifiable evidence must place the related transaction into an exception state unless an authorized temporary treatment exists.
- A financial record must not be approved solely by the actor who created or supplied the evidence when segregation-of-duties rules require independent review.
- The same evidence must not be reused to support multiple financial effects unless each effect is explicitly valid and the allocation is documented.
- Evidence retention must follow the active financial retention policy and remain accessible for reconciliation, dispute handling, audit, and migration.
- Exported evidence references must remain resolvable without changing the meaning of the underlying financial record.
- Deletion of evidence linked to a posted or approved financial record is prohibited; invalid evidence must be superseded or marked invalid with preserved history.
