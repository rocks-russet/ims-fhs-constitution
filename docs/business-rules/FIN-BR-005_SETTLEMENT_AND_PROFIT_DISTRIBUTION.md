# FIN-BR-005 — Settlement and Profit Distribution

Purpose: Define when sale proceeds, capital recovery, expenses, and profit become financial rights.

Rules:

- Settlement occurs only after the related order reaches `COMPLETED`.
- Payment confirmation alone does not recognize realized profit.
- The settlement calculation must use the final authoritative values of the completed order.
- Order settlement must distinguish at least:
  - item revenue;
  - discounts and additional charges;
  - recognized inventory cost or capital recovery;
  - marketplace or transaction fees;
  - buyer shipping charged;
  - actual shipping cost;
  - shipping reimbursement;
  - shipping surplus or deficit;
  - realized profit.
- Shipping surplus or deficit is part of realized order profit.
- Actual shipping cost paid by the shipping handler must be reimbursed to the handler wallet as part of settlement.
- Capital recovery and profit distribution must follow the ownership snapshot stored on each inventory item.
- Later changes to ownership rules must not retroactively change completed settlement results.
- Multi-item orders must calculate and preserve item-level settlement before aggregation.
- The sum of all wallet postings generated by settlement must equal the final settlement result.
- Settlement must be idempotent and must not be posted more than once for the same completed order.
