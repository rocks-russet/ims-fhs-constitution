# FIN-BR-055 — Financial Close Checklist

Purpose: Define mandatory completion gates before a reporting period may be marked CLOSED.

Rules:
- Confirm all eligible sales, purchases, refunds, withdrawals, reimbursements, and settlements are posted.
- Reconcile wallet balances to the financial ledger.
- Resolve or formally carry forward unidentified funds and suspense items.
- Review failed, duplicate, reversed, and pending financial events.
- Confirm inventory valuation and cost-of-goods-sold consistency.
- Record approved accruals, deferrals, impairments, and period adjustments.
- Verify owner capital and profit-right balances.
- Generate and preserve close reports and reconciliation evidence.
- Record preparer and independent reviewer approval.
- Lock the period only after all mandatory checks pass or approved exceptions are documented.

Invariants:
- A close cannot rely solely on report generation.
- Unresolved differences must be disclosed.
- The checklist result is retained as an auditable close artifact.
