# CON-BR-013 — Contact Tags and Classification

## Feature Origin

Contact List and Contact Settings.

## Purpose

Support filtering and operational grouping.

## Rules

1. Tags are descriptive metadata and do not replace roles or lifecycle status.
2. Tags use an approved registry.
3. Duplicate tags are normalized or merged.
4. Tag assignment and removal are auditable.
5. Inactive tags cannot be newly assigned.
6. Tags may support customer/supplier segmentation and operational grouping.
7. Tags cannot independently authorize or block a transaction.
8. Sensitive or unsupported accusatory labels are prohibited.

## Invariants

- Tags do not determine Contact identity.
- Historical tag changes remain traceable.
