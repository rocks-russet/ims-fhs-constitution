# SAL-BR-031 — MANUAL SALES ADJUSTMENT

## Purpose
Define governance for manual sales adjustment.

## Rules
- Every action must reference the originating Sales Order.
- Inventory, payment, shipment, and financial consistency must be preserved.
- Historical records are immutable; corrections use governed workflows.
- Actor, timestamp, and reason are mandatory.
