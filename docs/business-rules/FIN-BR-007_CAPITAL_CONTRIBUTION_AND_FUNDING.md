# FIN-BR-007 — Capital Contribution and Funding

Purpose: Govern the recognition of funds contributed to IMS FHS and distinguish capital from revenue, profit, and temporary custody.

Rules:
- A capital contribution is recognized only when funds or another approved economic resource are irrevocably provided for IMS FHS business use.
- Every capital contribution must identify:
  - contributor Wallet Code;
  - amount;
  - currency;
  - contribution date;
  - funding source or payment reference;
  - receiving Settlement Account, when applicable;
  - purpose or designated bucket;
  - actor and approval evidence.
- A capital contribution must be posted to the contributor's financial rights and the designated wallet bucket according to the approved funding arrangement.
- Capital contributions must not be recorded as sales revenue or realized profit.
- Funds received temporarily for safekeeping, settlement, reimbursement, or pass-through payment are not capital contributions.
- A contribution assigned to the FHS wallet must specify whether it belongs to `OPERATING_CASH`, `TRADING_CAPITAL`, or another future approved bucket.
- Non-cash contributions require an approved valuation basis and a traceable reference to the contributed asset.
- A promised contribution creates no wallet effect until the contribution is actually received and accepted.
- Returning contributed capital requires a separately authorized withdrawal, distribution, or capital-return transaction; the original contribution entry remains immutable.
- Capital contribution postings must be idempotent and reconcilable to external payment evidence or the accepted non-cash asset record.
