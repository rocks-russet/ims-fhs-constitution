# Canon

The Canon defines concepts that should rarely change.

CANON-001 IMS FHS is the internal operating system of FHS.

CANON-002 Inventory is the primary source of truth.

CANON-003 Business Rules take precedence over implementation.

CANON-004 Wallet represents financial rights, not bank balances.

CANON-005 Audit history is preserved by default.

CANON-006 Reality First: software follows the business process.

CANON-007 Operator First: reduce operator workload without sacrificing integrity.
